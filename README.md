## Tracking Pitfalls and How to Avoid Them

This is the part most "topcashback ticketmaster" guides gloss over, and it's where most missing cash back actually happens. The cash-back tracking cookie is fragile. Here's what kills it, in roughly descending order of frequency:

- **Ad blockers and privacy extensions.** uBlock Origin, Ghostery, Privacy Badger, Brave Shields, and similar tools block the tracking pixel that tells TopCashback you made a purchase. Disable them for the entire purchase session, or use a clean browser profile just for cash-back shopping.
- **Coupon extensions like Honey, Pouch, or rival cash-back add-ons.** These hijack "last click" attribution and quietly steal the commission. Turn them all off before you click through TopCashback.
- **Using a voucher code that didn't come from TopCashback.** Any outside code overrides the tracking link. Stick to codes listed on TopCashback's Ticketmaster page.
- **Rejecting cookies on Ticketmaster.** Cash-back tracking literally depends on cookies. Reject them at the consent prompt and there's nothing for TopCashback to record.
- **Switching devices or browsers mid-purchase.** Click through on desktop, finish on desktop. Don't start on your laptop and check out on your phone.
- **Closing the tab and coming back later.** Finish the entire order in the original session. If the session expires, start over from TopCashback.

If cash back never tracks despite doing everything right, you can file a "missing cash back" claim from your TopCashback Earnings page. Keep your Ticketmaster order confirmation email — you'll need the order number, date, and amount. Claims usually take several weeks to resolve because TopCashback has to go back to Ticketmaster's network to confirm the purchase.
